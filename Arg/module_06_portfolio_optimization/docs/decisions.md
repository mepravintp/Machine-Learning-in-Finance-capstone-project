| M6-02 | Which "traditional Markowitz" | Minimum risk (variance) · maximum Sharpe | **Maximum Sharpe** (the tangency portfolio), long-only, max 25% per stock; minimum risk shown as a one-line note | Maximum Sharpe is the textbook version and uses both inputs (expected returns and co-movement).
