MODULE 1 · CORPORATE FINANCE

Which strategic initiatives should get capital?

ML weak · NPV decides
What the Board needs decidedFor each of the 180 proposed projects: should NovaFin Fund, Review or Reject it, and why?
Our answerFund 21, Review 18, Reject 141. Of the 21, the best 5 get ₹487 cr in the final ₹1,000 cr plan (all low-risk, at least 90% chance of success).

THE DATA · what history shows
Low risk rating = clearest sign of success
Low-risk initiatives succeeded 89% of the time vs ~70% for the rest. Investment size, GDP and rates showed no link.
THE MODEL · what ML can predict
Simple beats complex, but prediction is weak
With just 42 failures, flexible models memorise noise. A 3-variable logistic model is best, but AUC 0.63 is modest, so ML is a risk check, not a picker.
THE MONEY · what is worth funding
Finance does the filtering: 180 → 5
NPV over 5 years at 12%, minus the loss if a project fails, ranked by value per rupee. The Fund list's realised success is 86% vs 76% for rejects.
THE RISK · what could change the answer
It all depends on how long projects earn
Over 3 years nothing pays back; over 7 years the list doubles. Cutting every success odds by 10 points still leaves 12 good projects.
So what for the Board
  • Fund on value, not on model scores. NPV does the filtering; the model only adjusts for failure risk.
  • Ask for a Low risk rating and a growing segment. They are the only traits that reliably raise success odds.
  • Release money in stages. The value relies on projects earning in years 4–5, so stop funding if a project falls behind plan.
